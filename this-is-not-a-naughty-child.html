Case study 02 · Parenting guide (non-fiction)

This Is Not a Naughty Child

A cover, interior and Amazon KDP launch for a psychologist's parenting guide on ADHD. The goal: earn a worried parent's trust in one glance, then stay easy to read when they are tired.

In short

Sulaimon Qudus designed the cover, formatted the interior and handled Amazon KDP publishing for This Is Not a Naughty Child: How to Understand a Child with ADHD and Rebuild Connection by psychologist Ewa Downing. The book is live on Amazon.

01 / The challengeWhat the book needed

Parenting books have a different job from novels. The reader is often stressed, short on time and looking for someone they can trust. If the cover looks cheap or the pages look like a school handout, that trust is gone.

Ewa is a psychologist, so her expertise had to show on the cover straight away. Inside, the pages needed to feel calm and easy to follow. And when the files were ready, the book still had to get through Amazon KDP correctly, which is where many self-published authors get stuck.

Readers: Parents of children with ADHD.
Tools: InDesign, Photoshop, Canva, Word.

02 / The approachHow it was designed

  1. A cover that tells a story of connection

    A parent's hand on a child's shoulder at sunset, with a glowing light and brain motif that hints at ADHD without labelling the child. Calm, hopeful and human.

  2. Authority on the front

    "Psychologist and Author" sits under Ewa's name and a small Psychology for Parents mark adds credibility, so parents know this book comes from real expertise.

  3. A clear title hierarchy

    The main promise, This Is Not a Naughty Child, is large and bold. The subtitle explains exactly what the reader gets: how to understand a child with ADHD and rebuild connection.

  4. An interior made for tired readers

    Generous margins, comfortable line spacing, clear chapter openers with number, title and subtitle, and highlighted callouts in the introduction so key ideas are easy to find again.

  5. Amazon KDP publishing, handled

    Title page, copyright page and table of contents set up properly, files prepared for print and Kindle, then uploaded and published on Amazon. The listing displays correctly on desktop, tablet and phone.

05 / The outcomeWhere the book is now

This Is Not a Naughty Child is live on Amazon. Ewa went from a finished manuscript to a published product page with one partner handling the cover, the interior and the KDP upload.

The trust built on this project led to a second one. Ewa came back for her 504-page novel, The Last Land.

Liveon Amazon in paperback and Kindle
3 servicescover, formatting and publishing in one project
Repeat clientEwa returned for her next book

06 / For your bookWhat you can take from this

For a parenting book, the cover's real job is trust. Everything else follows from that.

If you write non-fiction, put your credentials where buyers can see them and keep the inside calm and scannable. Readers of practical books want to find answers fast.
