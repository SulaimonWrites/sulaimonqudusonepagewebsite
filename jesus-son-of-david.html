Case study 05 · Christian fiction and Bible study workbook

Jesus "Son of David"

A golden, painterly cover for a re-imagined biblical story, extended into a 60-page companion workbook that helps readers and church groups go deeper.

In short

Sulaimon Qudus designed the cover for Jesus "Son of David": A Re-imagined Short Story by Jimi Fawehinmi, plus a 60-page companion workbook with six study modules, reflection questions, scripture connections, prayer pages and a weekly planner.

01 / The challengeWhat the book needed

Faith-based readers want more than a story. They want to reflect, pray and apply what they read, often together with a church group or book club.

Jimi's book retells the story of King David, the blind man Bartimaeus and his cry for mercy, with a view into the spiritual battle behind the scenes. He wanted readers to go deeper. That meant a second product, a workbook, that had to feel like part of the same family as the book.

Readers: Christian readers, small groups and Bible study leaders.
Tools: Canva, Photoshop, Word.

02 / The approachHow it was designed

  1. A cover painted in light

    Warm gold, painterly art: Jesus walking toward Bartimaeus, King David with his harp, an angel in light, and darkness at the edges hinting at spiritual warfare. A thin, elegant serif title keeps it reverent.

  2. A workbook cover in the same family

    The workbook uses the same art world with the word workbook in a flowing script and the author's name running down the side, so the two books clearly belong together on a shelf or a church table.

  3. Six modules that follow the book

    The Calm Before the Storm, The Shepherd Boy and the Scenes Behind, Rumblings, David, Bathsheba and Uriah, Mount Zion, and Jerusalem. Each module lines up with a chapter of the story.

  4. A repeatable study rhythm

    Every module has a summary of key points, reflection questions, scripture connections, an action step and space for prayer and journaling. Readers know what to expect and can work at their own pace.

  5. Built for groups as well as individuals

    An introduction explains how to use the workbook alone or in a group, with a tip for group leaders. Extras include memory verses, character study prompts, final reflection questions and a weekly planner.

05 / The outcomeWhere the book is now

Jimi now has two products instead of one: the story, and a study tool for personal devotion, Bible studies and book clubs. The workbook gives churches a simple reason to use the book in groups.

The matching covers make the pair easy to present and easy to sell together.

06 / For your bookWhat you can take from this

A workbook turns a book people read once into a book groups use for weeks.

If your book teaches or inspires, think about a companion workbook or journal. It adds value for readers, opens doors with churches and groups, and gives you a second product to sell.
