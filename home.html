The real problem

You spent months writing it. Readers decide in seconds.

On Amazon, your cover is a small thumbnail between dozens of others. If it looks homemade, most readers never click to see how good the story is.

01

The cover blends in

A cover that does not match its genre, or cannot be read at thumbnail size, gets scrolled past. No click, no sale.

02

The inside looks unfinished

Uneven spacing, messy chapter pages and no running heads tell readers the book was rushed, and it shows in the reviews.

03

Publishing feels like a maze

Trim sizes, bleed, spine width, file rejections. KDP can turn the last step into the most stressful one.

I take care of how your book looks and how it gets published, so you can focus on writing and selling it.

How it works

A simple process. No surprises.

You always know what is happening, what comes next and what I need from you.

1

Tell me about your book

Send a short brief on WhatsApp or email: title, genre, audience and what you need. I reply with questions, a clear quote and a timeline.

Your part: a few details and covers you like
2

Research and direction

I study the best sellers in your exact genre so your book fits the shelf and still stands out. Then I share the design direction before going deep.

Your part: approve the direction
3

Design and formatting

Cover concepts, interior layout, illustrations or A+ Content, built in InDesign and Photoshop to your printer's exact specifications.

Your part: feedback on drafts
4

Proof and polish

We review everything together and refine until it feels right. I check spacing, spine width, bleed and every page before the files are final.

Your part: final sign-off
5

Publish and launch

You get print-ready and Kindle-ready files plus mockups for promotion. If you choose publishing support, I help you go live on Amazon KDP.

Your part: tell the world

What does Sulaimon Qudus do?

Sulaimon Qudus is a book cover designer, interior formatter and Amazon KDP publishing assistant for self-published authors. He designs ebook, paperback and hardcover covers, typesets interiors for print and Kindle, illustrates children's books, creates Amazon A+ Content and designs companion workbooks.

How much does a book cover or formatting project cost?

It depends on what your book needs: an ebook cover only, a full print wrap, interior formatting, illustration or publishing support. Send a few details about your book on WhatsApp or by email and you will get a clear, fixed quote before any work starts. No hidden extras.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the scope. A cover is quicker than a fully illustrated picture book, and a 500-page novel takes longer to typeset than a short guide. You will get a clear timeline with your quote, and progress updates along the way.

Can you publish my book on Amazon KDP for me?

Yes. As a KDP publishing assistant, Sulaimon prepares your print and Kindle files to Amazon's specifications and helps you upload and publish, so your book goes live correctly. This Is Not a Naughty Child by Ewa Downing is one book published this way.

Do you format books for both print and Kindle?

Yes. Interiors are built in Adobe InDesign for print, at the trim size you choose (for example 6 x 9 inches), and prepared as a Kindle-ready ebook file as well.

Do you illustrate children's books?

Yes. Children's book projects can include character design, full colour illustrated spreads, page layout with text placement, the cover and Amazon A+ Content. See The Cat Who Saved a Queen case study for an example.

What is Amazon A+ Content and do I need it?

Amazon A+ Content is the set of images and text modules that appears in the From the Publisher section of your Amazon product page. It lets you show your characters, key benefits and author story visually. It is a strong way to help browsers become buyers, especially for children's books and non-fiction.

Which genres do you work with?

Children's picture books, parenting and self-help, dark fantasy romance, science fiction and adventure, Christian fiction and faith-based workbooks. Each cover is designed to fit what readers of that genre expect to see.

Do you work with authors outside Nigeria?

Yes. Sulaimon works remotely with authors anywhere in the world, and the books in this portfolio were created for authors based in the UK and Europe. Communication is by WhatsApp and email.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your final manuscript (for formatting), your book title and subtitle, author name, genre, trim size if you know it, your back cover blurb, and two or three covers in your genre that you like. If you are not sure about any of these, that is fine. We can work it out together.
