The Last Land

A 504-page, 90,000-word novel typeset for 6 x 9 inch print, with a deep-ocean cover that sets up the mystery before page one.

In short

Sulaimon Qudus designed the cover and typeset the full interior of The Last Land by Ewa Downing: about 90,000 words across 504 pages, three parts and seventeen chapters, formatted for a 6 x 9 inch paperback.

01 / The challengeWhat the book needed

Long novels expose every formatting shortcut. Across 500 pages, small problems like uneven spacing, broken headers or a messy contents page add up, and readers start to feel the book is amateur even if they cannot say why.

The story itself mixes genres: thriller pace, sci-fi technology and a hidden world under the ocean. The cover had to promise all of that in one image.

Readers: Adult readers of sci-fi, mystery and adventure.
Tools: InDesign, Photoshop, Word.

02 / The approachHow it was designed

  1. A cover that pulls you under

    A deep teal ocean palette and the silhouette of a sunken city. The title pairs a script The with a bold LAST LAND, and the hook line from the back cover appears on the front: beyond the surface lies another world.

  2. A clear structure for a big book

    Dedication, opening quote and a full table of contents with page numbers, then three part openers, Part One: The Sleeping City, Part Two: The Walking World and Part Three: The First Light, each with its own epigraph.

  3. Running heads that guide the reader

    Facing pages carry the author's name and the book title in spaced capitals, with page numbers, so readers always know where they are in a 504-page book.

  4. Body text set for long reading

    Comfortable type size, line spacing and margins for a 6 x 9 inch trim, with consistent chapter openers across all seventeen chapters.

  5. A spine that works on a shelf

    The spine carries the title and author clearly, sized for the real page count so the print file is accurate.

04 / DeliverablesWhat was delivered

  • Front cover, spine and back cover
  • 6 x 9 inch print interior, 504 pages
  • Table of contents with page numbers
  • Three part openers with epigraphs
  • Running headers and page numbering
  • 3D mockup for promotion

05 / The outcomeWhere the book is now

The Last Land reads smoothly from the first page to The End. The structure helps readers move through a long, layered story, and the cover sets the mood before they open it.

This was the second book for Ewa, after the parenting guide This Is Not a Naughty Child. When an author trusts you with a non-fiction book and then a 500-page novel, the work speaks for itself.

504pages typeset for print

06 / For your bookWhat you can take from this

In a long novel, good formatting is invisible. Readers only notice it when it is missing.

For long books, ask your formatter how they handle running heads, part openers and the contents page. Those details decide whether a 500-page book feels professional or tiring.
